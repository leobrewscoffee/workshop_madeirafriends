Why Specialty Coffee?

When we talk about specialty coffee, we're speaking of a commitment to excellence at every stage—from seed to cup—ensuring the coffee's unique flavors truly shine.

This involves meticulous care throughout the entire journey:

  • From the farm: Careful selection of varieties, sustainable agricultural practices, and precise processing methods.
  • To the roaster: Expert roasting that highlights the inherent characteristics of the bean.
  • To your cup: Water quality and brewing method play crucial roles.

A key differentiator for specialty coffee is its scoring. Coffees are professionally 'cupped' and graded. Anything scoring 80 points or above on a 100-point scale by certified Q Graders is considered specialty coffee. The difference between high-quality specialty beans and lower-grade commercial beans is remarkable—you can see it, smell it, and taste it!

Brewing Methods Deep Dive

☕ Moka Pot

Strong, concentrated brew with full-bodied flavor and bold profile.

Key Steps: Pre-heat water, use medium-fine grind, fill basket gently (never tamp!), use low heat, and remove from heat as soon as coffee gushes.

ParameterDetail
Ratio~1:7 (concentrated)
GrindMedium-fine
Time3-5 minutes

☕ French Press

Fast, simple method yielding a wonderfully full-bodied and immersive cup.

Key Steps: Use medium-coarse grind, steep for 3-5 minutes, push plunger down slowly, always decant immediately to prevent over-extraction.

ParameterDetail
Ratio1:15
GrindMedium-coarse
Time3-4 minutes

☕ V60 Pour-Over

The most versatile method allowing subtle flavors and delicate aromas to shine.

Key Steps: Heat water to 90-96°C, rinse paper filter, use three-pour technique (Bloom, Second, Third) to control extraction.

ParameterDetail
Ratio1:15
GrindMedium
Time3-5 minutes
